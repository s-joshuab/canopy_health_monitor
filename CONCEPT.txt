CANOPY HEALTH MONITOR
Simple Concept

THE IDEA
Canopy Health Monitor is a dashboard that helps people see the condition of forests and tree-covered areas in Region 1. It brings maps, warnings, satellite images, and data summaries together in one place.

THE GOAL
Help PENRO R1 staff notice places that may need a closer look. The app does not replace visiting and checking a location in person. It is a guide for planning and deciding where to start.

PRIMARY CLIENT AND USERS
PENRO R1 is the intended client. The main users are its environmental and field staff, who can use the dashboard to track forest conditions and plan where to follow up.

COVERAGE
Region 1 only: Ilocos Norte, Ilocos Sur, La Union, and Pangasinan.

HOW IT HELPS
- The dashboard shows summary numbers and changes in the condition of monitored areas.
- The map makes it easier to compare provinces.
- Warnings point out places that may have changed and should be checked.
- Satellite image details and reports are kept together in one place.

CURRENT STATUS
The app is still a prototype. Most numbers, warnings, and records are sample data and are not connected to a live monitoring system. The image shown is a real NASA GIBS Terra/MODIS reference image of Pangasinan, but it is not the same Sentinel-2B image described by the sample record beside it. Official data and in-person checks must be connected and verified before the app is used to make real decisions.
